Official Safety Protocols & Community Standards by Rukamafun

Welcome to Rukamafun, your trusted guide for online classifieds safety. Whether you are browsing listings, communicating with users, or arranging local meetings, following our verified safety rules ensures a secure and scam-free environment.

Important Safety Notice: Always exercise caution when contacting users or buying items through online classifieds. Never transfer money in advance or share sensitive credentials.

Essential Safety Rules & Best Practices

  • Protect Personal Details: Never share sensitive personal information such as bank passwords, OTPs, or government IDs.
  • Meet in Safe Public Places: Always choose well-lit, busy public locations (e.g., shopping centers, coffee shops) for in-person pickups.
  • Verify Before Paying: Inspect items thoroughly in person before releasing any payment or completing transactions.
  • Avoid Upfront Money Transfers: Refuse requests for advance deposits, wire transfers, or gift card payments.
  • Use Secure Communication: Keep initial conversations inside official messaging channels until trust is established.
  • Report Suspicious Activity: Immediately report fake ads, spam attempts, or inappropriate conduct to platform moderation.

Rukamafun Community Code of Conduct

  • Maintain polite, honest, and respectful communication at all times.

  • Comply fully with local laws, regulations, and platform terms of service.
Pro Tip from Rukamafun: If an offer seems too good to be true, it usually is. Trust your instincts and report fraudulent listings immediately.
